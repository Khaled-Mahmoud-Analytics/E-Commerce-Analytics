
# E-Commerce Business Performance Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

Sales are only part of the story when analyzing an e-commerce business.

The real challenge is understanding how much value those sales generate and how business performance translates into profitability.

This project presents an **Interactive Power BI Dashboard** designed to analyze e-commerce business performance, covering sales, profitability, product performance, sales channels, branches, and geographical regions.

The main objective is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that support better management decisions.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Business Performance
- Total sales and profit increased by 7.4%.
- Sales quantity grew by 7%.
- The business maintained a relatively stable relationship between sales and profitability.

### 2. Product Performance
- Product P0002 generated the highest sales at 17.7M, but its profit was only 1.9M.
- Product P0009 generated 17.1M in sales and achieved the highest profit at 2.2M.
- P0002 also accounted for the largest share of total discounts among the five products analyzed.

**Business Insight:** The product generating the highest revenue is not necessarily the most profitable. Product costs and discount strategies should be considered when evaluating product performance.

### 3. Sales Channel Performance
- Website contributed approximately 39% of total sales.
- Instagram contributed approximately 30%.
- Website and Instagram together generated approximately 69.5% of total sales.

**Business Insight:** These channels represent a significant share of revenue. However, marketing budget allocation should also consider channel profitability and customer acquisition costs.

### 4. Regional & Branch Performance
- Delta and Greater Cairo together contributed approximately 67.5% of total sales.
- Branches showed relatively close sales and profit performance.

**Business Insight:** Further analysis of operating costs and branch-level profitability could help identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency.

## Business Perspective

Beyond reporting numbers, this project focuses on answering important business questions:

- Are the highest-selling products also the most profitable?
- Are discounts increasing profitability or reducing profit margins?
- Are the highest-revenue sales channels delivering the best returns?
- Which regions and branches offer opportunities for performance improvement?
- Where can the business reduce costs and improve profitability?

The goal is to move from simply reporting what happened to understanding why it happened and identifying opportunities for better business decisions.
